1	Learning Objectives 
The final project for students registered in EEEC 1099 will be a literature review extended from the materials that we have covered in the class.  The general expectation is that you will pick a topic, collect 1 – 3 additional readings (I can suggest some, and the literature just needs to be representative not comprehensive), and write a report around 4 - 5 pages (double space).  The project title should be a question, and the report is to answer that question.  There are three general areas that you can write the report on:  
•	Dig into the existing biosensors and try to understand the detailed design and operational tradeoffs or make comparison using the class materials. 
•	Focus on a particular choice of biosensors for in-depth analyses, especially on the tradeoffs within design objectives.
•	Focus on a biomedical or physiological question and discuss how to use biosensors for improved understanding and diagnostics.

2	Sample Projects
•	How to improve conventional ECG with multiple leads?
•	What additional information can we get by combining ECG, PPG and cuff-based blood pressure sensors?
•	How can we improve the emotion and stress evaluation by HRV?
•	Why are non-oscillometer-based blood pressure sensors unreliable?
•	How should we make concise suggestions to cardiopulmonary patients for the medical imaging exams?

4.	Report Guidelines 

The report should be around 4 – 5 pages for two persons in a group, or 6 – 7 pages for three persons in 11-pt double-space 1-inch-margin A4-size pages with everything included (figures, tables, references, etc.)  The length is a suggestion, and will not take any penalty for over length.  All figures and tables from literature or web pages should be clearly and explicitly referenced in the figure caption.  The reference should follow strictly the IEEE reference format.  
The project report should have the following elements:

•	Title (limited to one line and should be a question)
•	Members and NYCU ID/email
•	Objectives and overall coverage for the question in the title
•	Main discussions
•	References (IEEE format)

You are welcome to start from GPT or similar tools, but each of you need to understand the full content of the report during the oral exam.  If your answer is: “I do not know what that means as it is from GPT” or “That is done by my group member so I cannot answer it”, you will receive a 0 for the oral exam part.

For file submission, please name your project report as: LastName_LastNam_FinalProject.  Submit your project file in Word or pdf on E3.
